EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases

effLarge.ts is derived from the "EFF Large Wordlist" by the Electronic
Frontier Foundation (Joseph Bonneau, 2016):

  https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases
  https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt

Changes: the dice numbers were removed, and the four hyphenated words
(drop-down, felt-tip, t-shirt, yo-yo) were left out. The list is otherwise
unchanged. scripts/build-wordlist.mjs reproduces it from the original file.
